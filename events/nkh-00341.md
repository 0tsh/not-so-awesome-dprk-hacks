This campaign, dubbed “No Pineapple”, focused on intelligence-gathering, starting with an attack on a company that was exploited through CVE-2022-27925 (remote code execution) and CVE-2022-37042 (authentication bypass) – two vulnerabilities affecting the digital collaboration platform Zimbra. ... used Dtrack malware, an information-stealing backdoor, to eventually steal 100 GB of data.”"
