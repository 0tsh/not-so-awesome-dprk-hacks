    text: "The Lazarus group continues to update its TTPs (Tactics, Techniques and Procedures) to help it fly under the radar. We would urge organizations involved in the booming crypto-currency or technological startup industry to exercise extra caution when dealing with new third parties or installing software. It’s best to check new software with an anti-virus program or at least use popular free virus-scanning services such as VirusTotal . You should never set ‘Enable Content’ (macro scripting) in Microsoft Office documents received from new or untrusted sources. If you need to try out new applications, it’s better to do so offline or on an isolated network virtual machine which you can erase with a few clicks. For more details on this and other research, you can subscribe to our APT intelligence reports ."
