      Andariel is suspected to have stolen 1.2TB worth of data in addition to extorting money from the affected companies. The collected ransom is believed to have been laundered via the bank account of an individual only identified as Ms. A. by law enforcement. Police in South Korea seized servers, cell phones and laptops allegedly used by the group and searched the apartment of the woman, described as a foreigner."
